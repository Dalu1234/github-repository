About

Building machines that understand space, physics, and the world in 4D.

Background

I'm Chukwudalu Dumebi-Kachikwu — researcher, builder, and student leader at the University of Maryland. My work lives at the intersection of spatial intelligence, physics simulation, and embodied AI. Most of my time goes toward one question: how do we build systems that understand the world the way it actually is — in 3D, in motion, under physical constraints?

What I'm thinking about

At UMD, I lead STAR Labs, a research group building physics-grounded 4D world models using anisotropic Gaussians as a unified geometry-physics-rendering substrate. I also serve as the Project Lead at BigThink AI, UMD's largest AI student organization, serving as the Project Manager for projects as well as designing and teaching workshops to make machine learning and AI universally adopted.

Across everything I build, the obsession is the same: close the gap between simulation and reality. I'm drawn to problems where the right representation unlocks everything else — and convinced that spatial intelligence is the next frontier.
